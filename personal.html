PERSONAL ESSAY · LIFE & GROWTH 01

Everything All at Once!

What happens when the life you dreamed of turns out to be nothing like you imagined?

My first job took me from Kerala to Ahmedabad, alone, with big ambitions and plenty of confidence. Reality had other plans. This is a story about uncomfortable beginnings, homesickness, adapting to change, and discovering that sometimes the biggest transformation happens within us.

Read the summary ↓

The story behind the story

My journey began with a three-day train ride from Kerala to Ahmedabad for my first corporate job. Excited to live independently, travel and meet successful people, I was unprepared for the challenges that awaited me.

Language barriers, difficult accommodation, illness and loneliness made me question whether I belonged there. Many times, I wanted to quit and return home. It was a difficult introduction to living independently.

Gradually, things began to change. Friendships, small improvements in my living situation, movies, prayer and discovering Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu helped me find my footing. I started to feel more confident, even though life wasn't suddenly perfect.

The biggest lesson was that the situation around me had not changed as much as I had. I was learning to adapt, face discomfort and trust myself. What initially felt impossible eventually became an experience that shaped me.

“This too shall pass.”
