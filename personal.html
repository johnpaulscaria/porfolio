01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

People first.
Always learning.

I enjoy being out in the world — meeting people, hearing their stories, exploring unfamiliar places and connecting ideas that don’t usually sit next to each other.

That’s part of why I was drawn to business development: it brings together communication, problem-solving and the chance to understand how different people and organisations work.

I’m also interested in philosophy and the way people make decisions. I don’t have every answer; I like asking better questions.
